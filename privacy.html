Privacy Policy

India Run: Legends of Bharat · Saurabh Eduverse · Last updated 18 November 2026

India Run is a family-friendly running game made by Saurabh Sharma (Saurabh Eduverse). This policy explains what the game does with information. In short: we do not collect, store or share any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Game progress saved on your device

Your coins, unlocked cities, chosen runner, treasures, daily challenge progress and settings are saved only on your own phone, inside the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Optional player name and challenge links

In Multiplayer you can type a nickname and create a "Challenge a friend" link. The link contains only your nickname, score, city and the path of your run, so your friend can race your ghost. The link is created on your phone and is shared only when you choose to send it (for example on WhatsApp). We do not receive or store these links.

Internet permission

The app includes the standard internet permission so that challenge links and the share button can open other apps such as WhatsApp or your browser. The game itself runs fully offline.

Children

The game is suitable for children. Because no personal information is collected, no information about children is collected either.

Third-party content

3D characters are Microsoft Rocketbox avatars (MIT licence). The 3D engine is three.js (MIT licence). Monuments are original models inspired by real Indian landmarks.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
